# Subway Simulation

Graph-based subway travel with interior ride and GPS-correct exits.

## Modules

- `nyc_world/city/subway.py` — 6 stations, `SubwaySimulation`, commuter NPC use in rain

## Player experience

Stand near a station, press **E**, pick destination **1–6**, ride the car interior, emerge at the correct world position.
