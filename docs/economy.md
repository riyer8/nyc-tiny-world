# Pillar 10 — City evolution & economy

Weekly business simulation drives facade changes and an evolution journal.

## Modules

- `nyc_world/simulation/economy.py` — 12 Washington Square businesses, residences, weekly tick

## Player experience

Fast-forward time (twin 30×). Shops may close and reopen under new names. Press **J** for the evolution journal.

## Persistence

`economy` field in save files. `WorldState.economy` snapshot.
